The Urban Heat Challenge

Modern cities are becoming heat islands. Glass facades, concrete expanses, and poor urban design trap heat, making our daily walks uncomfortable and unhealthy. As temperatures rise, the way we move through cities has become a climate and health issue.

We can't redesign cities from scratch. But we can use what's already there — buildings, shadows, and greenery — to create healthier paths through urban heat.

What is ShadePath?

ShadePath uses GIS technology, weather data, and building geometry to calculate shaded walking paths in real time. It transforms the harshness of the city into livable, walkable, and healthy routes.

By understanding urban shadows and green infrastructure, ShadePath guides you through the coolest, most comfortable paths available — turning every walk into a way to move smarter through the heat.

How It Works

ShadePath operates on three intelligent layers that work together to create optimal walking experiences

🏢

Urban Shade

Urban geometry and building shadows are mapped in real-time, calculating shade coverage throughout the day to identify the coolest walking corridors.

🌿

Green Infrastructure

Trees, parks, and vegetation are integrated into route planning, prioritizing paths that maximize exposure to natural cooling and air quality benefits.

📱

Smart Platform

GIS data, real-time weather information, and intuitive user experience combine to deliver personalized, climate-aware navigation.

Beyond the Map

ShadePath isn't just a navigation tool — it's a new way of thinking about urban flow, sustainable design, and ESG micro-interventions. Every route calculation contributes to a larger understanding of how cities can adapt to climate change.

By revealing the hidden cooling networks that already exist in our cities, ShadePath helps urban planners, residents, and policymakers see new possibilities for sustainable urban living.

This is urban intelligence designed for the climate era — where technology serves both individual comfort and collective resilience.
